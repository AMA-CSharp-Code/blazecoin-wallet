BLAZECOIN WALLET

Privacy Policy — Android, iOS and web wallet apps

Effective date: 2026-08-23

The short version: Blazecoin Wallet is a non-custodial wallet. Your keys are created and stay on your device. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no ads, and no trackers. We never collect your name, email, or any identity information — the app has nowhere to even type them.

1. Who we are

Blazecoin Wallet is published by the Blazecoin project. Questions about this policy: admin@blazecoin.co.uk.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your recovery phrase and keys — generated on the device and stored in the platform's secure storage (Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage; the iOS Keychain; an encrypted, password-protected vault in your browser for the web wallet). They are never transmitted, never backed up by us, and never visible to us.
  • Your settings and convenience data — display preferences, address book, labels, watch-only addresses, and your PIN (stored only as a salted hash). All local, never uploaded.
  • Biometrics — fingerprint/face unlock is performed entirely by your device's operating system. The app only receives a yes/no answer and never sees biometric data.

The Android app opts out of Android's cloud/device-transfer backups, so your wallet data is not copied off the device by the OS backup system.

3. What leaves your device

To show balances and send coins, the app talks to Blazecoin gateway servers (operated by the project) over HTTPS. Those requests necessarily contain:

  • the wallet addresses being looked up (balances, history, unspent coins),
  • signed transactions you choose to broadcast, and
  • your device's IP address, as with any internet request.

The app cryptographically verifies what the gateways return, so the gateways are an availability service, not a trusted party. If you prefer not to use them at all, the app supports a personal node mode that talks only to your own Blazecoin node, and a peer-to-peer broadcast fallback that sends transactions directly to the public Blazecoin network.

Blazecoin is a public blockchain. Transactions you broadcast become permanently public on the network by design — that is how every blockchain works, and it is outside any operator's control.

4. What our servers see and keep

  • Gateway servers process requests to serve them and apply per-IP rate limiting to prevent abuse.
  • Standard, short-lived operational logs (service journals) may record request metadata. They are used only to keep the service running and diagnose faults.
  • We do not build profiles, do not link addresses to identities, do not sell or share any data with third parties, and run no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services — on the servers or in the apps.

5. App permissions

  • Camera — used only when you open the QR scanner to read a Blazecoin address. Frames are processed on the device and never stored or uploaded.
  • Photos/files — only if you choose to pick a saved QR image instead of using the camera; the chosen image is decoded on the device.
  • Biometric hardware — see section 2.

6. Your data, your control

  • Deleting the wallet in-app, or uninstalling the app, removes all locally stored wallet data from the device. (Your coins live on the blockchain and are recoverable anywhere with your recovery phrase — which is also why we can never recover it for you.)
  • We hold no account or identity data about you, so there is nothing for us to export or erase on request beyond the transient operational logs described above.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and is intended for users old enough to enter into financial transactions in their jurisdiction.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised before the change ships.
